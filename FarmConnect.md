**FARMCONNECT (\*\*PHARMCONNECT\*\*)**  
This network shall connect farmers and sellers who shall get a good deal. It shall run on blockchain technology.

The idea is for farmers to obtain good prices for their produce by connecting the services to various people on the network. Farmers can obtain contracts on the network to grow specific crops which can then be grown up beforehand. The consumers can pay beforehand for the products and ensure the farmers get a good price. Farmers will grow crops on-demand with the money required to grow the food.

1. Customers make orders onto farm connect or say make bids for how much they will consume  
2. Farmers go on the network and make bids for how much they are willing to produce  
3. The suppliers deposit money on the network which is held by the network  
4. The farmer accesses the various items required to produce the products and grows them  
5. Network supervises the farmers product to ensure they are working and on truck  
6. Network ensures the produces receives their products in time  
7. Everyone goes home happy

To ensure sustainability and dependability, there is a need to boost production of the farmers who will have joined as farmConnect farmers by providing solutions to challenges that will impact on production such as drought, pests and other unknown challenges. One of the ways to do this is for farmConnect to lobby for funds that will be used to develop the capacity production and those that will be used to solve challenges that are known for example irrigation solutions to farmConnect Farmers Groups e.g community irrigation schemes that benefit farmConnect farmers in a specific region.

We can also boost agriculture by encouraging production zones. For example if an area or region is very good a specific crop and they maximize production of that crop to meet market demand.

There will also be a need to improve our quality so as to set the standard. FarmConnect can set the standard for crops required and also help link farmers to experts who will provide their expertise to farmConnect farmers.
